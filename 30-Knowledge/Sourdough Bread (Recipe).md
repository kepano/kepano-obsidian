# Sourdough Bread (Recipe)

Uses [[Sourdough Starter]].

## Tools

- Banneton — https://a.co/d/eeeIe29
- Dutch oven — https://a.co/d/82MMCTr

## Ingredients

- 600 g bread flour
- 150 g wheat flour
- 15 g salt
- 150 g starter
- 500 g water

## Method

1. Mix flours and salt well.
2. Add starter and water, mix until no dry flour remains.
3. Cover and rest 1 hour.
4. Stretch and fold every 30 minutes, 4 times. On the 4th time, pull the dough thin and push with a finger — it shouldn't break; if it does, fold again.
5. Flatten the dough and let it rise until doubled — about 4-5 hours in summer, 13 hours in winter.
6. Cut dough in half for 2 loaves. Shape per reference video.
7. Scrape into banneton (rice flour dusted first).
8. Put banneton in a plastic bag, tie a knot, refrigerate 12 hours.
9. Preheat oven to 450°F with the Dutch oven inside for 20 minutes, then remove.
10. Rest dough out of the fridge for 5 minutes, place on parchment paper, score with one confident cut.
11. Bake in the Dutch oven, lid on, 25 minutes at 450°F.
12. Remove lid, bake another 25 minutes.
13. Remove bread, cool on a wire rack.
14. Bake the second loaf the next day.
15. Cool completely — 1-2 hours — before cutting.
