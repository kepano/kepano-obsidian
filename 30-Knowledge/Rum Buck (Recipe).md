# Rum Buck (Recipe)

A cocktail recipe, related to Praneet's interest in [[Kombucha & Salgam|home fermenting and drinks]].

## Ingredients

- 1.5 oz. (45 ml) Hamilton's 86 Demerara Rum
- 1/2 oz. (15 ml) St. Elizabeth Allspice Dram (as a float)
- 1/4 oz. (7.5 ml) Fresh lime juice
- 4-5 oz. (120-150 ml) Bundaberg Ginger Beer
- Lime wheel for garnish

## Method

Fill glass with ice, build ingredients, float the Allspice Dram on top. Garnish with a lime wheel.
