# Old Fashioned (Recipe)

A cocktail recipe (Jamaican rum variant), related to Praneet's interest in [[Kombucha & Salgam|home fermenting and drinks]].

## Ingredients

- 1/4 oz demerara syrup
- 4 dashes Angostura bitters
- 2 fl oz (jigger) Jamaican rum
- 1 orange slice
- 1 maraschino cherry

## Method

Stir with ice for a few seconds, then pour over one large ice piece. Garnish with orange slice and maraschino cherry.
