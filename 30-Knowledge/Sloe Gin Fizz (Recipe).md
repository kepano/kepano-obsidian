# Sloe Gin Fizz (Recipe)

A cocktail recipe, related to Praneet's interest in [[Kombucha & Salgam|home fermenting and drinks]].

## Ingredients

- 1.5 oz sloe gin
- 1 oz lemon juice, freshly squeezed
- 0.75 oz simple syrup
- Club soda, to top
- Lemon and cherry for garnish

## Method

Add sloe gin, lemon juice, and simple syrup to a cocktail shaker with a handful of ice. Shake until icy-cold. Strain into a highball or Collins glass filled with ice. Top with club soda and garnish with lemon and cherry.
