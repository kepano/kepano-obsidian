# Sourdough Starter

Feeds Praneet's [[Sourdough Bread (Recipe)|sourdough bread]] baking.

## Tools

- Starter kit — https://a.co/d/ioLKqOq
- Instant-read thermometer — https://a.co/d/4FbsGH2

## Flour

- All-purpose flour
- Bread flour (King Arthur)
- Stone-ground rye (Bob's Red Mill)

## Starter build schedule

- **Day 1**: jar weight 343 g. 100 g flour, 150 ml water, 85°F. Date the starter bottle. Wait 24 hours.
- **Day 2 (first feeding)**: discard down to 70 g from previous day. Add 50 g stone-ground rye, 50 g unbleached all-purpose flour, 115 g water at 85°F. Stir, let sit 24 hours.
- **Day 3**: repeat day 2.
- **Day 4**: same as day 3, except water is 100 g.
- **Day 5**: same as day 4.
- **Day 6**: reduce starter to 50 g, rest same as day 5.
- **Day 7**: reduce starter to 25 g, rest same as day 6.
- **After day 7**: feed every 24 hours, forever.

## Ongoing weekly feeding

- 25 g starter + 50 g wheat flour + 50 g all-purpose flour + 100 g water.
- Leave out 2 hours, then refrigerate.
- Do this every week.
- First few days after starting: feed every 8 hours — starter should double or triple each time.
