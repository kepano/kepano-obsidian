# Mai Tai (Recipe)

A cocktail recipe, related to Praneet's interest in [[Kombucha & Salgam|home fermenting and drinks]].

## Ingredients

- 1 oz. (30 ml) Appleton 12yr Rare Blend Jamaican Rum
- 1 oz. (30 ml) La Favorite Ambre Rhum Agricole Ambre
- 1/2 oz. (15 ml) Kōloa Kaua'i Dark Rum
- 3/4 oz. (15 ml) Pierre Ferrand Dry Curaçao
- 1 oz. (30 ml) fresh lime juice
- 1/2 oz. (15 ml) orgeat syrup — reference: "4 EASY to Make Cocktail Syrups" video (link not captured, title only)
- Mint sprig, lime husk, and cocktail cherry for garnish

## Method

Build and garnish with an inverted lime husk holding a cocktail cherry. Float 151-proof rum on top and flame it.
