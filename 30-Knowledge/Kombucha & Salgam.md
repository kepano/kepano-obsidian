# Kombucha & Salgam

Praneet brews a batch of kombucha almost every week. He's also started making salgam, a fermented Turkish turnip/carrot drink. [[Aniket Gawade]] introduced him to kombucha-making.

## Kombucha recipe

- 1 gallon water
- 18 g black tea
- 1 cup sugar
- 2 cups SCOBY
