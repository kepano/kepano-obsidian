# Long Island Iced Tea (Recipe)

A cocktail recipe, related to Praneet's interest in [[Kombucha & Salgam|home fermenting and drinks]].

## Ingredients

- ½ fl oz vodka
- ½ fl oz rum
- ½ fl oz gin
- ½ fl oz tequila
- ½ fl oz triple sec (orange-flavored liqueur)
- 1 fl oz sweet and sour mix
- 1 fl oz cola, or to taste
- 1 lemon slice
- Mint leaves

## Method

Pour over ice and stir well until the ice level is even with the fluid. Garnish with lemon slice and mint leaves.
