# MNREC (NIT Allahabad)

Praneet's alma mater — attended as MNREC (Motilal Nehru Regional Engineering College); now known as MNNIT / NIT Allahabad (Motilal Nehru National Institute of Technology).

## Background

Established in 1961 as one of India's seventeen Regional Engineering Colleges, a joint Government of India / Government of Uttar Pradesh enterprise affiliated with the University of Allahabad. Named after Motilal Nehru; the foundation stone was laid by Jawaharlal Nehru. Started with one branch — Electrical Engineering — and was the first college in India to start an undergraduate Computer Science & Engineering program, in 1976–77. On June 26, 2002, MNREC became a National Institute of Technology and Deemed University, fully funded by the Government of India, and is now recognized as an Institute of National Importance.
