# Fitness

Praneet is into fitness — a recurring weekly routine.

## Log

## Daily exercise routine

### Warm up

1. 20 squats
2. Twists — touch hands, take one back all the way
3. High knees — 30
4. Butt kicks — 30
5. Squat dip — 10 seconds, shift to left foot then right
6. Hands on floor, raise hips

### 30-60-90 (3 rounds — rest 10 sec between exercises, 30 sec between rounds)

1. Gorilla burpee — lunge, lunge, squat, burpee — 30 sec
2. Sprawl frog kick, one or two at a time — 30 sec
3. Bear crawl hold, tabletop position, knees off ground — 30 sec
4. Dumbbell bicep curl and high pull, increase reps each round — 90 sec

### Second circuit (3 rounds)

1. Squat jumps — 2 mini jumps out and in, move forward/backward if possible — 30 sec
2. Spider monkeys — push-up position, lunge one leg all the way to the hand, alternate — 30 sec
3. Wide push-up hold — 30 sec
4. V-up and push-up, increase reps each round — 90 sec
